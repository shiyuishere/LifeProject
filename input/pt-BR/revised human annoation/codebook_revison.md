## Second meeting
### IR
**Clarification:**
- The idea of “doing something for someone” is no longer sufficient to qualify as IR.
- IR should only be used when the goal explicitly refers to relationships or closeness with another person (Reference example: 11100044 * locl 8)

**Additions:**
- Note 1: Goals expressing a general wish to spend time with or be closer to: friends, family members, loved ones, social circle should be coded ONLY under IR, when: no specific activity or concrete action is mentioned.
    Examples:
    “Spend quality time with my friends”
    “Improve the quality of my relationship with my roommates”
    “Go visit my parents”

**Rationale:**
- Distinguishes relational intent from other domains (e.g., leisure, travel, caregiving), ensuring IR remains focused on the relational bond rather than the activity itself.

### FS
**Revisions:**
- Updated rule to clarify boundary with `Hou`. Revised Text: "Buying or having one’s own space or place to live, without mentioning real estate, should be coded under Housing (HOU)." Implication: FS should not absorb goals related to acquiring a personal residence; FS applies only when housing acquisition is financial/investment-driven.
- Updated Note 1 to clarify overlap with `WEC`. Revised Text: "Goals related to obtaining a living from one’s occupation, getting a job with better pay, retirement with financial security, or increasing profit from one’s own business should also be coded under Work, Education and Career (WEC)." Implication: FS may co-occur with WEC in income-generating contexts; Financial goals tied to employment or business activity do not belong solely to FS.

## Third meeting
### Hou
**Clarifications:**
- Housing (HOU) is exclusively for goals related to living (residential purposes).
- It does NOT apply to goals about: having an office, building or acquiring workspaces, business-related properties

**Implication:**
- Office-related goals should be coded under Work, Education and Career (WEC) or Finances & Subsistence (FS) depending on context.

**Removed:**
"The meaning of “living in a place” was removed from HOU when tied to forming or sharing a household with a partner. These goals should now be coded under Marriage & Relationships (MAR) instead." - Reason: The underlying intention is relational/partnership formation, not housing improvement.

### HQL
**Clarifications:**
- The model previously failed to recognize that “having a comfortable place” relates to well-being.
- It was confirmed that comfort-related goals fall under HQL, not HOU—unless they involve actual housing improvements.

**Implication:**
- Comfort-oriented goals should be treated as well-being, not real estate or housing.

### CI
**Revisions:**
- Work for NGO: If the goal is to work for an NGO, it should be coded as: CI + WEC (occupational context)
- Volunteer for NGO: If the goal mentions volunteering, it should be coded only CI.

**Rationale:**
- Paid or professional work belongs to WEC.
- Volunteer activities reflect community engagement, not career development.

### FS
**Clarifications:**
- Buying land belongs to FS, not HOU. - Reason: Land purchase is a financial asset or investment unless there is explicit mention of residential living. Residential purposes would trigger `Hou` .

### WEC
**Clarifications:**
- Stopping working should be coded only under WEC. 

**Rationale:**
- The core intention involves career or employment status change, not financial, social, or retirement goals.

### EFT
**Exclusion**
- If a goal does not mention a specific activity, it should not be coded under EFT. Example: “I want more free time” → not EFT

**Clarifications:**
- EFT requires concrete activities (e.g., “learn painting”, “practice hiking”).
