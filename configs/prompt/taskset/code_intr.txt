# Category instructions

Each category corresponds to a life domain and is represented by a short code. Use the following codes for classification:

  - CI: Collective Issues
  - Dth: Death
  - EFT: Exploration and Free Time
  - FS: Finances and Subsistence
  - HQL: Health and Quality of Life  
  - Hou: Housing
  - IR: Interpersonal Relationships  
  - MAR: Migration and Area of Residence
  - MD: Mobility and Driving
  - Oth: Others
  - Pet: Pets
  - PDEI: Personal Development, Emotions and Identity
  - RS: Religion and Spirituality
  - Tra: Travelling
  - WEC: Work, Education and Career
